# Step 7.5 — Settlement replaces the interest run

**Status:** in progress · **Supersedes:** the interest run of
[07-interest.md](07-interest.md), and the "Liquidated" and ROI shapes of
[08-reports.md](08-reports.md) · **Evidence:** [legacy-findings.md](legacy-findings.md)

---

## Why

Reading the legacy program answered Q1–Q6 from code and data instead of from
memory, and two answers were the ones Step 7 said "would still cost code":
interest is **pro-rated by the day**, and the **rate is chosen per
settlement**. The station has never charged interest across every farmer at
once. It settles **one farmer at a time** when that farmer delivers palay. The
legacy screen calls this "Compute Percentage" and the button "Liquidate".

Decisions taken with Dan on 2026-10-15:

| Decision | Chosen |
|---|---|
| Interest | A per-farmer **settlement**, replacing the station-wide interest run |
| Deliveries | **As the legacy system does.** Recorded at the counter, applied to the balance only when a settlement includes them, interest first |
| Wording | Plain English: *advance slip* not voucher, *settlement* not liquidation, *payment* not receipt |
| Rename depth | Screens and docs only. Go identifiers and table names stay |

## The rule

```
interest on one advance  = principal × rate × days ÷ 30          rounded half-up to the centavo
days                     = cut-off date − advance date           (calendar days)
interest on carried      = carried principal × rate × days ÷ 30
days (carried)           = cut-off date − previous settlement's cut-off
```

The rate is a **monthly** rate in basis points (500 = 5% a month). It defaults
to the `interest.rate_bps` setting (500) and can be changed on each settlement.
That is simple interest: unpaid interest carried forward is owed but never
charged interest itself. This reproduces all 7,072 legacy liquidated advance
rows to the centavo.

## The documents

**Payment** keeps draft → posted → voided. What changes is what *posted*
means: a posted payment is **recorded**. It has its OR No. and a place in the
list, but it has **not yet moved the balance**. A payment is either cash (an
amount typed) or palay (lines of class, sacks, kilos and price per kilo, with
cost = kilos × price computed here and the amount being their sum).

Voiding a recorded payment that no settlement has applied writes nothing to
the ledger. A payment a settlement has applied cannot be voided. Void the
settlement first, which reopens it.

**Settlement** is one farmer up to one cut-off date. Its draft *is* the
preview, as the interest run's was:

- a line for each **open advance**, meaning a posted advance-slip line for this
  farmer dated on or before the cut-off and not in a posted settlement, with
  its days and interest
- a **carried** line for the principal left from before. That is the principal
  balance at the cut-off minus the open advances, clamped at zero, accruing
  from the previous settlement's cut-off, or from the farmer's first entry if
  there is none
- every **recorded, unsettled payment** for this farmer dated on or before the
  cut-off, split interest-first (per `payment.applies`) in date order against
  carried interest plus the interest just computed

**Posting** writes one `interest` entry for the total (if it is not zero),
sourced to the settlement, then one `payment` entry per included payment,
sourced to the payment, so a statement still reads "OR No. 812". All are dated
the cut-off. Posting recomputes inside the transaction and refuses a stale
preview, as the run did. A settlement needs at least one payment, as the
legacy Liquidate button did.

**Voiding** (manager only) reverses every entry the settlement wrote, which
reopens its advances and payments exactly as the legacy "Recall → Delete" did,
but for one farmer only. Only a farmer's **latest** posted settlement can be
voided. Otherwise the carried figures of the ones after it would describe a
history that no longer exists.

Settlement is counter work: create and post are open to encoders, and void is
a manager's.

## Reports

- **Return of Investment** keeps its portfolio block, and its table becomes the
  legacy one: one row per settlement in the period, with principal returned,
  interest paid and the amount applied.
- **Liquidated** becomes **Settlements**: every settlement in the period. Each
  row opens the settlement, which can be reprinted. That is the legacy
  "Recall".

## What is deliberately not done

- **Overpayment** still becomes credit on the principal. The legacy data
  suggests the surplus is paid out in cash on the day, but that is unconfirmed
  ([legacy-findings.md](legacy-findings.md#q2--how-farmers-repay)).
- **No O.R. number existed** in the legacy system. The OR No. sequence stays
  until the owner says whether a paper pad exists.
- **Per-customer default rates.** The rate is per settlement, as it was. Six
  farmers in eighteen years were settled at more than one rate, which does not
  justify a column.
